Why This Matters

All Service

The expansion of information, new technologies and AI have increased the demand for data at astronomical rates and trusted business intelligence depends on quality data flowing across systems now more than ever.

JLINC Answers:

Where did this data originate and from whom?
What was the information agreement of the data exchange across systems?
What errors or mishandling occurred and exactly where in the data supply chain?

The answers to these questions allow for accurate troubleshooting and error correction which results in data trust and integrity of systems.

Traditional logs can be edited or lost, JLINC adds an immutable, cryptographically signed knowledge chain so every data hand off across any system is provable. Â 

The need for a lightweight interoperable data provenance system to manage the traceability and auditability of data inputs and outputs becomes paramount for safely scaling business processes or artificial intelligence using private or regulated data requiring enterprise level compliance.

Reduce Data Risk

Reduce Data Risk

Significant business risk arises from not having a clear view of data, what came in, from where, for what purpose, and on what terms, and most importantly, where did it then go from here. JLINC enables the automated logging of data lineage and provenance with permissions, which allows data risk to be actively managed.

Demonstrate Compliance

Compliance with many of today's regulations require an audit trail that shows exactly how that data has been derived. JLINC automates this process by logging every data transaction, categorizing it and making that validation available for all relevant parties to inspect should they want or need to do so.

Build Customer Trust

Customers need transparency and control of their data to move beyond the technically and legally opaque models for data sharing most widely in use today. JLINC gives all parties both transparency and control before and after they share their data, thus providing a platform for truly trustworthy data exchange.
