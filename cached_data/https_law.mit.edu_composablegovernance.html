Collected Works on Composable Governance

Collected Works on Composable Governance is an open-access publication published by the MIT Computational Law Report. Submissions include original research, practical guides, tutorials, and overviews of topics related to composable governance. We convened several speakers to discuss the key composable governance themes and examples in June, and you can see the archived video and learn more about the speakers in our media section. Accepted submissions will be published in two batches. The first batch of submissions will be published in December 2022. To encourage a broad range of voices, topics were not limited to any particular length or format.

Building Net-Native Agreement Systems

by Joshua Z. Tan and Luke V. Miller
JT
LM
Published: Dec 05, 2022
The idea of agreement systems has permeated the bedrock of communities throughout recorded history. Since the advent of the internet, agreement systems have proliferated. This article provides a comprehensive examination of net-native agreement systems

Governance as Conflict: Constitution of Shared Values Defining Future Margins of Disagreement

by Eric Alston
EA
Published: Dec 05, 2022
Often viewed as a way to reduce conflict within society, the traditional perception of governance is one that maintains stability with minimal disturbance. This article thoroughly examines new possibilities enabled by using conflict as a tool for institutional decision-making.

Governance in DAOs: Lessons in Composability from Primate Societies and Modular Software

by Renita Murimi
RM
Published: Dec 05, 2022
This article gives a novel accounting of governance, exploring the concept of composability in primate societies and modular software with focuses on service-oriented design and consensus, cooperation, and conflict regulation.

by Nathaniel Williams
NW
Published: Dec 05, 2022
As one of the primary tools within the toolkit of governance throughout history, there are many different ways voting can be configured. This article explores the impact of digital technology on voting systems in the context of composable governance.
